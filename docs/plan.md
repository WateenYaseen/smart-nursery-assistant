# Smart Nursery Assistant — Project Plan

**Program:** IEEE WIE JS Mentorship Program 2026  
**Track:** AI & Programming  
**Project Stage:** Planning and Documentation

Smart Nursery Assistant is a responsive web application designed to help a nursery owner manage individual plants using image-based AI assistance. The system will identify supported plant types, assess visible plant conditions, provide relevant care and treatment guidance, and estimate watering needs qualitatively when possible. Each plant will have its own record containing previous inspections, images, and owner-confirmed care actions. The system will also suggest and prioritize next actions. It will communicate uncertainty clearly and will not claim to measure actual soil moisture or guarantee a diagnosis or treatment result. IoT and sensor-based monitoring are outside the initial version.
